What is Digitox?

Digitox locks your Android phone to one screen for a set time — 15 minutes to 8 hours — so you can focus, study, or sleep without distractions. No apps, no notifications, no way out except paying a small fee to unlock early. That real stake is what makes it actually work.

1. Open the app

A quick 3-step explainer, then tap Start Detox -- or apply a referral code first.

2. Set your intention

Pick a purpose (Deep Work, Study, Sleep...) and a duration from 15 minutes to 8 hours.

3. Phone locks itself

Screen pinning kicks in. No notifications, no app-switching, just the countdown -- until time's up.

4. Real money, real stakes

Leaving early means paying to unlock -- in real currency, via Google Play. That's what makes it work.

5. Track your progress

Streaks, minutes saved, success rate, and a 7-day session history -- all on-device.
